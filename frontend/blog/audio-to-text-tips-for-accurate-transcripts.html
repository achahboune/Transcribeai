Audio to Text: 7 Tips for More Accurate Transcripts

Better audio means a better transcript. These seven habits make the biggest difference.

Published 2026-10-11

1. Record in a quiet place

Background noise is the main cause of mistakes. Close windows, turn off fans and keep music low.

2. Keep the microphone close

A microphone near the speaker captures clearer speech than one across the room. A simple headset often beats a laptop microphone.

3. Speak clearly and at a steady pace

You do not need to speak slowly, but avoid rushing and avoid several people talking at once.

4. Choose the right language

If you know the language, select it instead of relying on detection, especially for short clips or mixed-language content.

5. Use a good file

Upload the original recording when you can, instead of a heavily compressed copy.

6. Proofread names and terms

Brand names, people and technical words are the most likely to need a quick fix. Skim the transcript once before sharing it.

7. Export in the right format

Use TXT for notes, Word or PDF for documents, and SRT or VTT for subtitles.
