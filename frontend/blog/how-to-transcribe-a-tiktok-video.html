How to Transcribe a TikTok Video to Text

Need the words from a TikTok video as text? Here is the quickest way to get a clean transcript.

Published 2026-10-11

What you need

A public TikTok video link, or the video file saved on your device, and a free TranscribeAI account.

Steps

  1. Open the TikTok video, tap Share, then Copy link.
  2. Open TranscribeAI and choose TikTok.
  3. Paste the link into the video link field.
  4. Pick the spoken language, or leave automatic detection on.
  5. Press Transcribe and wait for the text to appear.
  6. Copy the transcript, or export it if your plan includes the format you need.

If the link does not work

Private videos and videos removed by the creator cannot be transcribed from a link. If you have the video saved on your phone or computer, upload the file instead using the Upload a file area.

Tips for better accuracy

  • Clear speech with little background music gives the best results.
  • Choose the language manually if the video mixes languages.
  • Always proofread names, brands and technical terms.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free? The Free plan includes 300 minutes per month and TXT export. The Unlimited plan adds Word, PDF, SRT and VTT exports and an AI summary.

Can I transcribe other platforms? Yes, Instagram, Facebook and X public video links work too, and you can upload your own audio or video files.

Try it yourself

Paste a public video link or upload a file, and get your transcript in minutes. The Free plan includes 300 minutes every month, no credit card required.
