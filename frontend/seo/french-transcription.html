How it works

  1. Open TranscribeAI and paste a public supported video link.
  2. Choose a language or use automatic language detection.
  3. Start the transcription and review the generated text.
  4. Use the available export options for your plan when you need a file.

Availability depends on the source platform, public access and the current transcription service.

Who is this useful for?

Researchers can turn interviews and public videos into searchable notes. Creators can repurpose spoken content into written drafts. Students and professionals can make long-form video easier to review. Subtitle workflows can start from the same transcript instead of typing speech manually.

Frequently asked questions

Is the transcription free?

The Free plan includes 30 minutes per month, audio and video transcription, TXT export and no credit card requirement.

Can I transcribe any video?

No service can guarantee access to every video. TranscribeAI works with public supported links subject to platform availability and access rules.

Can I download subtitles?

Creator and Pro plans support SRT and VTT exports. TXT is available on the Free plan.
